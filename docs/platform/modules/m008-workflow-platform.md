Status

In Progress

Purpose

Provide workflow capabilities across the Eraxis platform.

The Workflow Platform establishes the foundation required for approvals, assignments, workflow tracking, and future business process execution.

The platform is designed to support reusable workflow functionality across Commerce, HRMS, Fleet Management, Inventory Management, Asset Management, and future Eraxis applications.
